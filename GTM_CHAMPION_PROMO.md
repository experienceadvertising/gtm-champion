# 🚀 GTM Champion: AI-Powered Strategy for Marketers

I've been working on a tool specifically for B2B/SaaS GTM marketers to automate the heavy lifting of strategy and content creation. It analyzes any company website in seconds to build a personalized execution plan.

### Key Features:
- **Instant Website Analysis:** Automatically scrapes and understands your business model, value prop, and GTM motion.
- **13-Channel Strategy:** Personalized recommendations across SEO, Paid Social, ABM, and more.
- **Weekly Content Sprints:** Actionable marketing tactics and ideas delivered fresh every week.
- **AI Content Generators:** One-click generation for LinkedIn posts, email campaigns, and SEO blog articles.
- **Performance Insights:** Real-time analysis of site speed and Core Web Vitals to optimize conversion.
- **Interactive AI Advisor:** A dedicated marketing assistant that knows your company context.

Built entirely with AI-assisted coding to help marketers move from "planning" to "doing" in minutes.
